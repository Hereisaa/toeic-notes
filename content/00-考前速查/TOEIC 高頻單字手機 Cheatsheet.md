## 公司與部門

- **headquarters** — 總部；corporate headquarters
- **branch** — 分公司；open a branch
- **department** — 部門；sales department
- **division** — 事業部；regional division
- **supervisor** — 主管；report to a supervisor
- **manager** — 經理；branch manager
- **assistant** — 助理；administrative assistant
- **policy** — 政策；company policy（insurance policy＝保單）
- **regulation** — 規定；comply with regulations
- **operations** — 營運；daily operations

## 行銷

- **promotion** — 促銷／升遷；sales promotion / get a promotion
- **advertisement** — 廣告；place an advertisement
- **launch** — 推出；launch a product
- **target market** — 目標市場；identify a target market
- **consumer** — 消費者；consumer demand
- **brand** — 品牌；brand awareness
- **slogan** — 標語；advertising slogan
- **discount** — 折扣；offer a discount
- **endorsement** — 代言／背書；celebrity endorsement
- **expand** — 擴展；expand into new markets

## 招募

- **recruit** — 招募；recruit staff
- **candidate** — 候選人；a suitable candidate
- **applicant** — 申請／應徵者；job applicant
- **vacancy** — 職缺／空房；fill a vacancy
- **résumé** — 履歷；submit a résumé（resume 作動詞＝恢復）
- **application** — 申請／申請表；submit an application
- **interview** — 面試；conduct an interview
- **hire / employ** — 僱用；hire an assistant / employ staff
- **orientation** — 新人訓練；attend orientation
- **background** — 背景；professional background

## 交通、住宿與安全

- **reservation** — 預訂；make a reservation
- **flight** — 航班；a connecting flight
- **accommodation** — 住宿；arrange accommodation
- **passport** — 護照；a valid passport
- **baggage / luggage** — 行李；不可數，two pieces of luggage
- **customs** — 海關；go through customs
- **boarding pass** — 登機證；show a boarding pass
- **transportation** — 運輸／交通；public transportation
- **evacuate** — 疏散；evacuate the building

## 生產與效率

- **efficiency** — 效率；improve efficiency
- **optimization** — 最佳化；process optimization
- **streamline** — 簡化、使更有效率；streamline the process
- **flexibility** — 彈性；scheduling flexibility
- **enhance** — 提升；enhance service quality
- **ensure** — 確保；ensure that… / ensure safety
- **exceed** — 超過；exceed expectations
- **thorough** — 徹底的；a thorough inspection
- **adjustment** — 調整；make an adjustment
- **in stock** — 有庫存；out of stock＝缺貨

## 專案與時程

- **deadline** — 截止日期；meet / extend a deadline
- **itinerary** — 旅行行程表；a revised itinerary
- **schedule** — 時程／排程；a delivery schedule
- **agenda** — 會議議程；review the agenda
- **seminar** — 研討會；attend a seminar
- **postpone** — 延期；postpone the meeting
- **delay** — 延誤；a shipping delay
- **reschedule** — 重新排定；reschedule for Friday
- **appointment** — 約定／任命；make an appointment
- **attend** — 出席；attend a meeting（不加 to）

## 提案與執行

- **proposal** — 提案；submit a proposal
- **initiative** — 新措施／主動性；launch an initiative / take the initiative
- **implement** — 實施；implement a policy
- **implementation** — 實施；implementation of the plan
- **strategy** — 策略；develop a strategy
- **workflow** — 工作流程；improve the workflow
- **install** — 安裝；install equipment
- **appoint** — 任命；appoint someone as director
- **authorize** — 授權；authorize someone to act
- **approve** — 核准；approve a request

## 信件與簡訊

- **request** — 請求；request assistance
- **Would you mind + V-ing…?** — 禮貌請求對方做事
- **do someone a favor** — 幫某人一個忙
- **appreciate** — 感謝／重視；appreciate your help
- **available** — 有空／可取得；available for a meeting
- **assist** — 協助；assist someone with a task
- **arrange** — 安排；arrange a meeting
- **confirm** — 確認；confirm a reservation

## 合約

- **contract** — 合約；sign a contract
- **agreement** — 協議；reach an agreement
- **negotiate** — 協商；negotiate terms
- **clause** — 條款；a cancellation clause
- **binding** — 具約束力的；legally binding
- **terminate** — 終止；terminate a contract
- **dispute** — 爭議；resolve a dispute
- **consent** — 同意；written consent
- **renewal** — 續約／更新；contract renewal
- **obligation** — 義務；fulfill an obligation

## 看題目最容易漏掉的 6 組

- **invoice / receipt**：請款文件／付款收據。
- **refund / reimbursement**：退購買款／報銷墊款。
- **eligible / available**：符合資格／可取得、有空。
- **complimentary / complementary**：免費招待的／互補的。
- **postpone / cancel**：延後／取消。
- **by / until**：最晚完成／持續到某時。
