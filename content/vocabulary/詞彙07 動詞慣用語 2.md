# 詞彙07 動詞慣用語 2

> [!info] 藍本 VOCABULARY Section 2〈慣用語〉Chapter 07。考「動詞 + 受詞 + 介系詞」的三段式搭配與商務高頻片語動詞，Part 5 每回約 1～2 題，Part 6 也常以片語動詞出題。

> [!abstract]- 本章重點
> 這一章的動詞後面先接受詞，再接固定介系詞帶出第二個名詞，寫成 provide A with B、inform A of B 這種模式。多益最常把「同一個介系詞的動詞群」放在一起考：空格給動詞，句子裡已經出現 A of B，就要選會接 of 的 inform、notify、remind。另一類是片語動詞（動詞 + 副詞或介系詞），意思常與字面不同，例如 turn down 是拒絕、carry out 是執行，必須整個當單字背。注意有些三段式動詞改成被動後，介系詞仍要保留：A is provided with B、A was informed of B。

## 必考清單
### 動詞 + A + with B
| 慣用語 | 中譯 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| provide A with B | 提供 B 給 A | The hotel provides guests with free Wi-Fi. |
| supply A with B | 供應 B 給 A | The farm supplies restaurants with fresh produce. |
| equip A with B | 為 A 配備 B | Each office is equipped with a projector. |
| present A with B | 頒發 B 給 A | The mayor presented the winner with a plaque. |
| replace A with B | 用 B 取代 A | We replaced the old server with a faster model. |
| compare A with B | 比較 A 與 B | Compare this quote with the one from last year. |
| combine A with B | 結合 A 與 B | The course combines theory with hands-on practice. |
| share A with B | 與 B 分享 A | Please share the report with the sales team. |

## 易混淆與注意
> [!warning] 注意
> - 接 of 的通知類動詞（inform、notify、remind、advise）改成被動後仍保留 of：Staff were **informed of** the change。
> - provide A with B 也可寫成 provide B for A，但不能寫 provide A B；supply 用法相同。
> - prevent、keep、prohibit、discourage 後面的 from 接 V-ing，不接原形動詞。
> - attribute A to B 的 to 是介系詞，B 是原因；be attributed to 常考被動。
> - consider A B 一般不加 as（consider him a leader）；regard A as B 的 as 不可省略。
> - compensate 與 reimburse 都接 A for B，但 reimburse 專指實報實銷的費用。
> - 片語動詞若受詞是代名詞，要放在中間：turn it down、fill it out、call it off。
> - take over（接管）與 take on（承擔、僱用）意思不同，turn in（繳交）與 turn out（結果是）也不同。
> - ascribe A to B 與 attribute A to B 意思相同，ascribe 語氣較正式，被動 be ascribed to 也常考。
> - dedicate A to B 的被動兼形容詞用法 be dedicated to（致力於）已固定成慣用語，見 [[詞彙05 形容詞慣用語]]；dedicate 與 devote 意思相近可互換。

## 實戰練習
1. Next month the company will ______ the old printers with energy-efficient models.
   (A) replace (B) notify (C) prevent (D) assign
2. Heavy snowfall ______ the delivery trucks from reaching the warehouse.
   (A) prevented (B) informed (C) attributed (D) replaced
3. The hotel ______ all conference attendees with complimentary wireless access.
   (A) provides (B) attributes (C) prevents (D) reminds
4. Employees will be ______ for all travel expenses incurred during the trip.
   (A) charged (B) reimbursed (C) blamed (D) assured
5. Because of low registration, the organizers decided to ______ the workshop.
   (A) call off (B) fill out (C) take over (D) keep up with

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (A) replace A with B 用 B 取代 A；notify 接 of，prevent 接 from，assign 接 to。
> 2. (A) prevent A from V-ing 防止；其他動詞不接 from。
> 3. (A) provide A with B 提供；attribute 接 to，prevent 接 from，remind 接 of。
> 4. (B) reimburse A for B 補貼費用；charged for 是收費，語意不合。
> 5. (A) call off 取消；fill out 填寫，take over 接管，keep up with 跟上。
