# 詞彙10 意義相近的動詞

> [!info] 藍本 VOCABULARY Section 3〈近義詞〉Chapter 10。考中文意思相近但用法不同的動詞，Part 5 每回約 2～3 題，Part 7 的同義詞題也常從這裡出。

> [!abstract]- 本章重點
> 近義動詞題的四個選項翻成中文幾乎一樣，判斷靠三件事：後面接什麼（受詞是人還是事、接 that 子句還是名詞）、及物或不及物、以及語氣正式程度。例如「通知」有 inform 與 announce，但 inform 後面接人，announce 後面接事情；「增加」有 raise 與 rise，但 raise 及物、rise 不及物。背的時候不要只記中文，要記「這個字後面接什麼」。

## 必考清單
### 1. 通知、告知
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| inform | 告知某人 | 人 + of / that | We informed clients of the new hours. |
| notify | 正式通知某人 | 人 + of / that | Notify HR of any address change. |
| announce | 宣布某事 | 事 + to 人 | The CEO announced the merger to staff. |
| advise | 建議或告知 | 人 + to V / of | We advise customers to book early. |

怎麼分：inform 與 notify 受詞是人，announce 受詞是事情；advise 多帶建議意味。

### 2. 增加、提高
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| increase | 數量增加（及物或不及物） | 名詞 / by | Sales increased by 8%. |
| raise | 提高（及物） | prices / funds / awareness | The store raised prices in June. |
| boost | 大幅提升 | sales / morale / productivity | The ad boosted sales overnight. |
| expand | 擴大範圍 | business / operations / into | The firm expanded into Asia. |
| enhance | 提升品質 | quality / reputation / image | Training enhances service quality. |
| rise | 上升（不及物，沒有受詞） | prices rose / sharply / by | Prices rose sharply last month. |
| soar | 大幅飆升（不及物） | prices / sales / costs soar | Fuel prices soared after the announcement. |

怎麼分：raise 一定要有受詞，rise 沒有受詞；expand 是範圍變大，enhance 是品質變好；soar 比 rise 語氣更強，指急遽飆升，兩者都不及物。

### 3. 減少、降低
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| reduce | 減少數量或程度（及物） | costs / waste / risk | We reduced packaging waste by half. |
| decrease | 減少（及物或不及物） | 名詞 / by | Complaints decreased last quarter. |
| lower | 降低價格、標準 | prices / rates / standards | The bank lowered its interest rates. |
| decline | 下滑（不及物）、婉拒（及物） | 無 / an offer | Profits declined for a third year. |

怎麼分：lower 專指價格與水準，decline 不接受詞時是下滑，接 offer 時是婉拒。

### 4. 檢查、審查
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| inspect | 實地檢查設施或貨物 | facility / equipment / shipment | Officials inspected the plant. |
| examine | 仔細檢視 | documents / evidence / patient | Auditors examined the records. |
| review | 審閱、重新檢視 | proposal / report / policy | Please review the attached contract. |
| monitor | 持續觀察 | progress / performance | We monitor sales daily. |

怎麼分：inspect 針對實體物品或場所，review 針對文件，monitor 強調持續一段時間。

### 5. 提供
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| provide | 提供（最通用） | 人 with 物 / 物 for 人 | We provide staff with uniforms. |
| offer | 主動提出 | 物 / to V / 人 物 | The hotel offers free parking. |
| supply | 供應（大量、持續） | 人 with 物 / 物 to 人 | The farm supplies milk to schools. |
| deliver | 送達、履行 | goods / results / speech | The courier delivered the package. |

怎麼分：provide 與 supply 都可接 A with B，supply 偏商業供貨；offer 帶「主動給」的意思。

### 6. 取得、獲得
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| obtain | 透過程序取得 | permit / approval / license | Obtain a permit before construction. |
| acquire | 收購、習得 | company / skill / property | The group acquired a rival firm. |
| gain | 逐漸獲得 | experience / access / popularity | She gained valuable experience abroad. |
| earn | 靠努力賺得 | salary / reputation / degree | He earned a reputation for honesty. |

怎麼分：obtain 用在文件與許可，acquire 用在收購與技能，earn 強調付出後得到。

### 7. 修改、修正
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| revise | 修訂文件內容 | report / estimate / plan | The estimate was revised upward. |
| modify | 局部修改 | design / terms / system | We modified the design slightly. |
| adjust | 微調以配合 | schedule / price / to | Adjust the thermostat to 22 degrees. |
| amend | 修正法規或合約 | contract / law / regulation | The contract was amended in May. |

怎麼分：revise 針對文字內容，amend 針對法律與合約，adjust 帶「配合情況微調」的意思。

### 8. 開始、成立
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| launch | 推出產品或活動 | product / campaign / website | We launched the app in April. |
| initiate | 發起程序 | program / discussion / process | The city initiated a recycling program. |
| establish | 設立組織、建立關係 | company / reputation / relationship | The firm was established in 1985. |
| commence | 正式開始（正式用語） | work / operations / on 日期 | Construction will commence on May 1. |

怎麼分：launch 用在產品與活動，establish 用在組織與關係，commence 是 begin 的正式說法。

### 9. 完成、達成
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| complete | 把工作做完 | project / form / training | Complete the form in block letters. |
| accomplish | 完成任務、達成目標 | task / goal / mission | The team accomplished its goal early. |
| achieve | 達成成果 | success / growth / target | We achieved record sales. |
| fulfill | 履行義務、滿足要求 | order / obligation / requirement | The supplier fulfilled the order. |

怎麼分：complete 接具體工作，achieve 接成果與目標，fulfill 接義務、訂單、要求。

### 10. 建議、要求
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| suggest | 提議 | V-ing / that 子句 | She suggested postponing the launch. |
| recommend | 推薦 | V-ing / that / 名詞 | I recommend the express service. |
| request | 正式請求 | 名詞 / that 子句 | We requested a copy of the invoice. |
| require | 要求、需要 | 名詞 / 人 to V / that | The job requires a valid license. |

怎麼分：suggest 與 recommend 後接 V-ing 或 that 子句，不接 人 to V；require 可接 人 to V。

### 11. 處理、解決
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| handle | 處理、應付 | complaints / inquiries / situation | Staff handle inquiries by phone. |
| address | 正視並處理 | issue / concern / problem | The memo addresses safety concerns. |
| resolve | 徹底解決 | dispute / issue / problem | The dispute was resolved out of court. |
| process | 按流程處理 | order / application / payment | Orders are processed within 24 hours. |

怎麼分：address 是著手處理，resolve 是解決完畢，process 是走流程。

### 12. 延後、取消
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| postpone | 延期到之後 | meeting / event / until | The meeting was postponed until Monday. |
| delay | 延誤、拖延 | shipment / flight / by | The flight was delayed by two hours. |
| cancel | 取消 | order / event / subscription | The event was canceled due to rain. |
| suspend | 暫停 | service / production / operations | Production was suspended for repairs. |

怎麼分：postpone 是改到之後的時間，delay 常是被動的延誤，suspend 是暫停而非取消。

### 13. 參加
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| attend | 出席（及物） | meeting / conference / class | All staff must attend the briefing. |
| participate | 參與活動（不及物） | in | Thirty firms participated in the fair. |
| join | 加入組織 | company / club / team | She joined the firm in 2019. |
| enroll | 註冊課程 | in a course / 人 in | Enroll in the workshop online. |

怎麼分：attend 直接接受詞，participate 一定接 in，join 是成為成員。

### 14. 選擇、決定
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| choose | 選擇（通用） | 名詞 / to V / between | Choose between two delivery options. |
| select | 從多個中挑選 | 名詞 / from | Select a date from the calendar. |
| determine | 確定、判定 | cause / whether / amount | Inspectors determined the cause of the leak. |
| opt | 選擇（不及物） | for / to V | Most clients opt for annual billing. |

怎麼分：select 比 choose 正式且暗示有清單可挑，determine 是查明或裁定，opt 一定接 for 或 to V。

## 易混淆與注意
> [!warning] 注意
> - raise 及物、rise 不及物；lay 及物、lie 不及物，多益都考過。
> - suggest 與 recommend 不接「人 + to V」，要寫 suggest that 人 (should) V。
> - attend 是及物動詞，attend to 是「處理、照料」，意思不同。
> - decline 接 offer 或 invitation 是婉拒，不接受詞是下滑。
> - 「獲得經驗」最常用 gain experience；acquire experience 也可以，obtain 不搭配。
> - address 當動詞時意思是「處理」或「向…發言」，不是「地址」。
> - postpone 與 delay 常互換，但 delay 可當名詞（a two-hour delay），postpone 不行。
> - achieve 與 accomplish 後面不接 to V。

## 實戰練習
1. More than 200 clients ______ the product launch in Taipei last week.
   (A) attended (B) participated (C) enrolled (D) engaged
2. The airline decided to ______ fares on all domestic routes.
   (A) rise (B) raise (C) grow (D) arise
3. Applicants must ______ a work permit before starting employment.
   (A) obtain (B) earn (C) achieve (D) accomplish
4. The consultant ______ that the company outsource its customer service.
   (A) reminded (B) suggested (C) told (D) allowed
5. Customer complaints are ______ by the support team within one business day.
   (A) handled (B) attended (C) participated (D) postponed

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (A) attend 是及物動詞直接接活動；participate、enroll、engage 都要接 in 才能接受詞。
> 2. (B) raise fares 及物；rise、grow、arise 都是不及物動詞。
> 3. (A) obtain a permit 取得許可證；earn 接薪資或名聲，achieve 與 accomplish 接目標。
> 4. (B) suggest that + S + 原形動詞；remind、tell、allow 後面都要先接人當受詞，不能直接接 that 子句。
> 5. (A) handle complaints 處理投訴；attend 接會議，participate 接 in，postpone 是延後。
