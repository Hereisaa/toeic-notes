# 詞彙12 意義相近的形容詞、副詞

> [!info] 藍本 VOCABULARY Section 3〈近義詞〉Chapter 12。考意思相近的形容詞與副詞，Part 5 每回約 2～3 題，Part 6 的副詞題也常從這裡出。

> [!abstract]- 本章重點
> 形容詞近義題看「修飾什麼名詞」：considerable 修飾數量，extensive 修飾範圍，都翻成「大量的」但搭配不同。副詞近義題看「放在哪裡、修飾什麼」：almost 修飾 all 或數字，approximately 只修飾數字；currently 描述現在狀態，通常搭配現在式或現在進行式。另外注意有些形容詞加 ly 後意思會變：hard 與 hardly、late 與 lately、high 與 highly，多益很愛考。

## 必考清單
### 1. 大量的、相當的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| substantial | 數量或程度可觀 | increase / amount / discount | We received a substantial discount. |
| considerable | 相當大的 | amount / time / effort | The project took considerable time. |
| extensive | 範圍廣泛 | experience / research / network | She has extensive experience in sales. |
| numerous | 為數眾多（可數） | occasions / complaints / requests | We received numerous requests. |

怎麼分：substantial 與 considerable 修飾量或程度，extensive 修飾範圍，numerous 只接可數複數名詞。

### 2. 合適的、適當的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| suitable | 適合某目的 | for / candidate / venue | The hall is suitable for large events. |
| appropriate | 合乎場合 | for / attire / action | Wear appropriate attire to the interview. |
| adequate | 足夠的 | supply / funding / space | There is adequate parking on site. |
| eligible | 有資格的 | for / to V / applicants | Only full-time employees are eligible. |

怎麼分：suitable 是適合用途，appropriate 是合乎規範或場合，adequate 是夠用，eligible 是符合資格。

### 3. 免費的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| free | 免費（通用） | of charge / shipping / sample | Shipping is free on orders over $50. |
| complimentary | 附贈的 | breakfast / ticket / copy | Guests enjoy a complimentary breakfast. |
| at no cost | 不需付費（片語） | to 人 | Repairs are made at no cost to the customer. |

怎麼分：complimentary 用在商家贈送的服務或物品，free 通用，at no cost 是副詞片語放句尾。

### 4. 有效的、有效率的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| effective | 有效果、生效 | method / immediately / as of | The policy is effective as of May 1. |
| efficient | 有效率、不浪費 | system / worker / energy | The new system is more efficient. |
| valid | 有效期內、合法 | ticket / passport / for | The ticket is valid for 30 days. |
| productive | 有生產力 | meeting / employee / day | We had a productive meeting. |

怎麼分：effective 是有效果，efficient 是省時省力，valid 是在期限內或符合規定。

### 5. 最近的、目前的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| recent | 最近發生的 | survey / years / study | A recent survey shows rising demand. |
| latest | 最新的 | version / issue / model | Download the latest version. |
| current | 目前的 | address / policy / rate | Update your current address. |
| upcoming | 即將到來的 | event / conference / release | Register for the upcoming seminar. |

怎麼分：recent 指過去不久，latest 是最新一版，current 是現在正在用的，upcoming 指未來。

### 6. 主要的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| main | 最重要的 | office / reason / entrance | Use the main entrance after 6 p.m. |
| major | 重大的、主要的 | change / client / city | We made major changes to the design. |
| primary | 首要的、最初的 | goal / concern / source | Safety is our primary concern. |
| leading | 領先的 | manufacturer / supplier / expert | We are a leading supplier of paper. |

怎麼分：main 與 primary 表「最重要」，major 表「重大」，leading 表「業界領先」。

### 7. 額外的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| additional | 額外增加的 | cost / information / staff | Additional fees may apply. |
| extra | 多出的（口語） | charge / copies / hours | There is no extra charge for delivery. |
| further | 進一步的 | information / notice / details | Contact us for further details. |
| spare | 備用的 | parts / key / time | Keep a spare key at the front desk. |

怎麼分：additional 與 extra 意思相同但 additional 較正式，further 是「更多、進一步」，spare 是備用。

### 8. 可能的、潛在的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| likely | 很可能（接 to V） | to V / it is / that | Prices are likely to rise. |
| possible | 有可能、做得到 | as soon as / it is / solution | Reply as soon as possible. |
| potential | 潛在的（修飾名詞） | customer / risk / buyer | We contacted potential buyers. |
| prospective | 未來可能成為的 | client / employee / tenant | Prospective tenants may view the unit. |

怎麼分：likely 常接 to V，possible 不接人當主詞，potential 與 prospective 只放在名詞前。

### 9. 熟練的、有資格的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| skilled | 有技術的 | worker / labor / at | We need skilled technicians. |
| experienced | 有經驗的 | in / staff / highly | She is an experienced negotiator. |
| proficient | 精通（技能、語言） | in / user / highly | Applicants must be proficient in Excel. |
| qualified | 具備資格的 | for / candidate / highly | He is highly qualified for the role. |

怎麼分：skilled 強調技術，experienced 強調經歷，proficient 用在語言與軟體，qualified 強調證照與資格。

### 10. 暫時的、臨時的
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| temporary | 暫時性的 | position / closure / staff | The road closure is temporary. |
| tentative | 暫定、未確定 | schedule / date / plan | The launch date is tentative. |
| provisional | 臨時、待確認 | agreement / license / results | A provisional agreement was reached. |
| interim | 過渡期間的 | manager / report / period | An interim manager was appointed. |

怎麼分：temporary 是時間上短暫，tentative 是尚未確定，interim 是正式人選到位前的過渡。

### 11. 副詞：幾乎、大約
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| almost | 幾乎（修飾 all、every、數字） | all / every / finished | Almost all staff attended. |
| nearly | 將近 | 數字 / complete / every | Nearly 300 people registered. |
| approximately | 大約（只修飾數字） | 數字 / 時間 | The trip takes approximately two hours. |
| roughly | 粗略大約 | 數字 / half | Roughly half the budget is spent. |

怎麼分：almost 與 nearly 可接 all、every，approximately 與 roughly 只接數字。

### 12. 副詞：目前、立即、最後
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| currently | 目前（現在式、進行式） | is / are / being | The store is currently closed. |
| immediately | 立刻 | after / contact / effective | Contact security immediately. |
| promptly | 迅速、準時 | respond / at 時間 / reply | Please reply promptly. |
| eventually | 最終、終於 | 過去式 / will | The dispute was eventually resolved. |
| shortly | 不久後 | after / before / will | The manager will arrive shortly. |

怎麼分：currently 描述現況，immediately 與 promptly 表「馬上」但 promptly 帶「準時」意味，shortly 指不久的將來。

### 13. 副詞：此外、因此、然而
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| additionally | 此外 | 句首 + 逗號 | Additionally, parking is free. |
| moreover | 而且（加強） | 句首 + 逗號 | Moreover, the price includes delivery. |
| therefore | 因此 | 句首 / 句中 | We therefore recommend the upgrade. |
| however | 然而 | 句首 + 逗號 / 句中 | However, the offer expires Friday. |
| otherwise | 否則 | 句首 + 逗號 | Pay by Friday; otherwise, a fee applies. |

怎麼分：這五個都是連接副詞，不能直接連接兩個子句，前面要有句號或分號，見 [[文法13 副詞]]。

## 易混淆與注意
> [!warning] 注意
> - hard（努力地）與 hardly（幾乎不）、late（遲）與 lately（最近）、high（高）與 highly（非常）意思完全不同。
> - considerable（相當大的）與 considerate（體貼的）拼字相近。
> - economic（經濟的）與 economical（節省的）、industrial（工業的）與 industrious（勤奮的）常混淆。
> - respective（各自的）與 respectable（值得尊敬的）、respectful（有禮的）三者不同。
> - almost 是副詞，不能直接修飾名詞：almost employees 錯，almost all employees 對。
> - approximately 只放在數字前，不能修飾 all 或 every。
> - effective immediately 與 effective as of + 日期是公告常用語，表示「即日起生效」。
> - later（之後）與 latter（後者）、former（前者）拼字相近。

## 實戰練習
1. The new warehouse offers ______ space for the expanded inventory.
   (A) adequate (B) eligible (C) numerous (D) skilled
2. ______ all of the survey respondents said they shop online weekly.
   (A) Almost (B) Approximately (C) Very (D) Much
3. The merger is ______ to be completed by the end of the year.
   (A) possible (B) potential (C) likely (D) prospective
4. A ______ manager will oversee the department until a permanent replacement is hired.
   (A) tentative (B) potential (C) provisional (D) interim
5. The office is ______ closed for renovation and will reopen on June 3.
   (A) shortly (B) currently (C) eventually (D) promptly

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (A) adequate space 足夠的空間；eligible 修飾人，numerous 接可數複數，skilled 修飾人。
> 2. (A) almost all 幾乎全部；approximately 只能修飾數字，不能修飾 all，very 與 much 不修飾 all。
> 3. (C) be likely to V 很可能；possible 不接 to V，potential 與 prospective 只放名詞前。
> 4. (D) interim manager 過渡期間的代理主管，與「直到找到正式人選」呼應；tentative 修飾計畫或日期，potential 是潛在的，provisional 修飾協議或結果。
> 5. (B) currently 目前，搭配 is closed 現在狀態。
