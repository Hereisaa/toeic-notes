# 文法04 動詞與主詞的單複數一致

> [!info] 藍本 Section 2〈動詞片語〉Chapter 04。動詞的單複數必須跟真正的主詞一致。多益 Part 5 每回約 1～2 題，常與時態混考，出題手法是把主詞和動詞隔開、或用看起來像複數的單數主詞。

## 基礎文法

單數主詞配單數動詞，複數主詞配複數動詞。規則本身簡單，難的是「找出真正的主詞」與「判斷主詞到底是單數還是複數」。

| 主詞 | 現在式動詞 | be 動詞（現在／過去） | have |
|---|---|---|---|
| 單數名詞、he／she／it | 動詞 + s | is／was | has |
| 複數名詞、they | 原形 | are／were | have |
| I | 原形 | am／was | have |
| you | 原形 | are／were | have |

- The invoice **is** attached.／The invoices **are** attached.
- Each employee **has** a locker.／All employees **have** lockers.

判斷步驟：（一）把主詞後的修飾語劃掉，找到真正主詞；（二）確認主詞單複數；（三）只有現在式與 be 動詞、have 需要變化，過去式（was／were 除外）與助動詞後的原形不變。

## 必考文法

### 考點 1：主詞與動詞被修飾語隔開時，找真正的主詞
介系詞片語、關係子句、分詞片語都不是主詞。把它們劃掉，剩下的名詞才決定動詞。

- The manager ~~of the sales teams~~ **is** on leave.
- The products ~~displayed in the lobby~~ **are** new models.
- The report ~~that the analysts prepared~~ **was** approved.

> [!warning] 注意
> along with、as well as、together with、in addition to 後面的名詞不算主詞：The CEO, along with the directors, **is** attending.

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主詞與空格之間有一長串 of 片語，選項考 is／are 或 has／have。主詞通常是句首那個名詞。

### 考點 2：一律當單數的主詞
下列主詞不管看起來多像複數，動詞都用單數：

| 類型 | 例 |
|---|---|
| each、every + 單數名詞 | Each department **has** its own budget. |
| everyone、everybody、anything、something、nobody | Everyone **is** required to attend. |
| 動名詞、to 不定詞、名詞子句當主詞 | Reducing costs **is** the priority. |
| the number of + 複數名詞 | The number of applicants **has** doubled. |
| 公司、組織、國家名稱 | Nexon Industries **is** hiring. |
| 學科、疾病、以 -s 結尾的單數名詞 | The news **was** unexpected. |

> [!warning] 注意
> each of + 複數名詞仍用單數動詞：Each of the branches **has** a manager。every 後接單數名詞，不接複數。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主詞是 each、every、everyone 或 the number of，選項有 is／are。看到這些字直接選單數。

### 考點 3：一律當複數的主詞
| 類型 | 例 |
|---|---|
| a number of + 複數名詞 | A number of employees **have** requested leave. |
| both、several、many、few + 複數名詞 | Several positions **are** still open. |
| A and B（兩個不同的東西） | The manager and the assistant **were** present. |
| police、people（永遠複數） | The police **are** investigating the complaint. |

> [!warning] 注意
> - a number of（許多）配複數，the number of（…的數量）配單數，兩者選項常同時出現。
> - staff、personnel 是集合名詞，多益採美式用法配**單數**動詞（The staff **is** well trained）；要表複數用 staff members。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格前是 a number of、several、both，選複數動詞。

### 考點 4：數量詞 + of + 名詞，看 of 後面的名詞
some、most、all、half、a lot、the rest、part、percent + of + 名詞，動詞看 of 後面的名詞單複數。

- Most of the equipment **is** new.（equipment 不可數）
- Most of the employees **are** new.（employees 複數）
- Half of the budget **has** been spent.
- One of the printers **is** out of order.（one of + 複數名詞，但主詞是 one）

> [!warning] 注意
> one of + 複數名詞 + 單數動詞，因為真正的主詞是 one。這是最常錯的組合。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主詞是 most of、some of、one of，看 of 後面（或 one）決定單複數。

### 考點 5：either…or、neither…nor 就近原則
用 either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also 連接兩個主詞時，動詞跟最靠近的那個主詞一致。

- Neither the director nor the assistants **were** informed.
- Either the managers or the CEO **is** going to sign.
- Not only the staff but also the manager **was** surprised.

> [!warning] 注意
> both A and B 是複數，不適用就近原則：Both the manager and the assistant **are** here.

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主詞含 neither…nor 或 either…or，看 or／nor 後面那個名詞。

### 考點 6：關係子句中的動詞與先行詞一致
關係代名詞 who、which、that 後面的動詞，單複數看先行詞。

- The employee **who works** in accounting has been promoted.
- The employees **who work** in accounting have been promoted.
- One of the printers that **are** on the third floor needs repair.

> [!warning] 注意
> one of + 複數名詞 + who／that 時，關係子句動詞跟複數名詞一致，主句動詞跟 one 一致。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格在 who／which／that 後面，選項是 is／are 或動詞加不加 s。往前找先行詞。

### 考點 7：there be 與倒裝句看動詞後面的主詞
there is／are 與地方副詞開頭的倒裝句，主詞在動詞後面。

- There **are** three vacancies in the department.
- There **is** a meeting at ten.
- Attached **are** the minutes of the meeting.

> [!warning] 注意
> Attached is／are、Enclosed is／are 是 Email 常見倒裝，動詞看後面名詞。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 句首是 There 或 Attached、Enclosed，選項有 is／are，看後面名詞。

## 實戰練習

1. The list of approved vendors ______ updated every quarter by the procurement office.
   (A) is (B) are (C) have (D) were
2. Each of the regional managers ______ responsible for submitting a monthly forecast.
   (A) are (B) is (C) have been (D) being
3. A number of customers ______ complained about the new checkout process.
   (A) has (B) is (C) have (D) was
4. Neither the invoice nor the receipts ______ included in the envelope.
   (A) was (B) were (C) has (D) is
5. One of the projectors in the training rooms ______ not working properly.
   (A) are (B) were (C) is (D) have

**解答與解析**
1. (A)：主詞是 The list，of approved vendors 是修飾語，單數選 is（考點 1）。
2. (B)：each of + 複數名詞仍用單數動詞 is（考點 2）。
3. (C)：a number of + 複數名詞配複數動詞 have（考點 3）。
4. (B)：neither…nor 就近原則，看 receipts，複數選 were（考點 5）。
5. (C)：one of + 複數名詞，主詞是 one，單數選 is（考點 4）。
