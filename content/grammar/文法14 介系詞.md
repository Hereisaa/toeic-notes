# 文法14 介系詞

> [!info] 藍本 Section 4 〈詞性〉Chapter 14。介系詞題考時間、地點介系詞的選擇，以及「介系詞與連接詞的區別」。Part 5 每回約 3～5 題，是文法題中題數最多、最需要背的一章。

## 基礎文法

介系詞後面接**名詞、代名詞或動名詞**，構成介系詞片語，在句子裡當形容詞（修飾名詞）或副詞（修飾動詞、整句）。介系詞後面**不能接子句**，這是它與連接詞的根本區別。

- The meeting **in the afternoon** was postponed.（修飾名詞 meeting）
- We will contact you **after the review**.（修飾動詞 contact）
- **Despite the delay**, the project finished on budget.（修飾整句）

### 時間介系詞
| 介系詞 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| at | 時刻、at noon、at night | at 3 p.m. |
| on | 日期、星期、特定日子 | on Monday、on May 5 |
| in | 月、年、季節、世紀、in the morning | in March、in 2026 |
| in | 多久之後 | The results will be ready **in** two weeks. |
| by | 期限之前（一次性動作） | Submit the form **by** Friday. |
| until、till | 持續到某時間點（狀態） | The store is open **until** 9 p.m. |
| for | 持續的時間長度 | for two weeks |
| since | 起點（配現在完成式） | since 2020 |
| during | 在某段期間內 | during the conference |
| within | 在期限內 | within 30 days |
| throughout | 整個期間 | throughout the year |
| from … to、through | 從…到 | from Monday through Friday |
| before、prior to | 之前 | prior to the launch |
| after、following | 之後 | following the merger |
| as of | 自某時起 | as of July 1 |
| on、upon + 動作名詞／V-ing | 一…就、當…時 | **upon receipt** of payment（見考點 6） |

### 地點與方向介系詞
| 介系詞 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| at | 點、地址、特定場所 | at the front desk、at 25 Main Street |
| in | 範圍內、城市、國家、房間 | in Tokyo、in the conference room |
| on | 表面、樓層、街道名 | on the third floor、on Main Street |
| between | 兩者之間 | between the two buildings |
| among | 三者以上之間 | among the staff |
| across from、opposite | 對面 | across from the bank |
| next to、beside、adjacent to | 旁邊 | adjacent to the lobby |
| near、close to | 附近 | near the station |
| along | 沿著 | along the river |
| through | 穿過 | through the main entrance |
| toward | 朝向 | toward the exit |
| throughout | 遍及 | throughout the region |
| within | 在範圍內 | within walking distance |

## 必考文法

### 考點 1：by 與 until、for 與 since、during 與 for
最常考的三組時間介系詞對比。

| 對比 | 差別 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| by vs until | by 是「在某時之前完成」（動作），until 是「持續到某時」（狀態） | finish **by** Friday；wait **until** Friday |
| for vs since | for + 時間長度，since + 起點 | for three years；since 2023 |
| during vs for | during + 事件名詞（在…期間），for + 時間長度 | during the meeting；for an hour |
| in vs within | in + 時間表「之後」，within 表「之內」 | in two weeks（兩週後）；within two weeks（兩週內） |

> [!warning] 注意
> - by 後面的動詞常是 submit、complete、finish、pay、respond 這類一次性動作。
> - until 後面的動詞常是 remain、stay、wait、be open 這類持續狀態。
> - during 後面不能接 while 那樣的子句。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格後是時間名詞，選項是 by、until、for、since、during。看動詞是一次性還是持續性選 by 或 until；看後面是長度還是起點選 for 或 since。每回 1～2 題。

### 考點 2：介系詞與連接詞的區別
兩者意思可以完全一樣，差別只在後面接什麼。判斷標準就一條：**看空格右邊有沒有「主詞 + 有時態的動詞」**。

```
介系詞 (prep.) + 名詞／代名詞／動名詞 (V-ing)
從屬連接詞 (conj.) + 完整子句 (S + V)
```

完整的三欄對照表（連接詞、介系詞、連接副詞）以 [[文法17 副詞子句]] 考點 1 為主場，這裡收介系詞側最常考的混淆組：

| 語意 | 介系詞（+ 名詞／V-ing） | 連接詞（+ S + V） |
|---|---|---|
| 因為 | because of、due to、owing to、on account of | because、since、as、now that |
| 雖然、儘管 | despite、in spite of、notwithstanding | although、though、even though、even if |
| 在…期間 | during、throughout | while、as |
| 除了 | besides、in addition to、apart from | — |
| 如果沒有 | without、but for | unless、if not |

三組必背對照：

- 因為：We stayed inside **because of** the heavy rain.（名詞片語）／ We stayed inside **because** it rained heavily.（S + V）
- 雖然：He won **despite** his injury.（名詞）／ He won **although** he was injured.（S + V）
- 期間：He fell asleep **during** the movie.（名詞）／ He fell asleep **while** he was watching the movie.（S + V）

**雙棲單字**：before、after、until、since、as 兩種身分都有，不能靠背詞性判斷，只能看後方結構。

| 單字 | 當介系詞（+ 名詞） | 當連接詞（+ S + V） |
|---|---|---|
| before | Wash your hands **before dinner**. | Wash your hands **before you eat**. |
| after | **After the meeting**, we revised the budget. | **After we met**, we revised the budget. |
| until | Wait here **until 5 P.M.** | Wait here **until the rain stops**. |
| since | She has worked here **since 2019**. | She has worked here **since she graduated**. |

> [!warning] 注意
> - despite of 是錯的；in spite of 才有 of。
> - due to 在正式寫法多接在 be 動詞後（The delay was **due to** a supplier issue），但多益也接受句首。
> - 動名詞算名詞，所以介系詞後面可以接 V-ing：**despite having** limited resources；**before submitting** the form。看到 V-ing 別誤判成子句。
> - 對等連接詞（FANBOYS：for、and、nor、but、or、yet、so）是另一類，連接的是「對等的兩端」而不是主從關係：apples **and** oranges、tired **but** happy、I like coffee, **but** she prefers tea。詳見 [[文法15 對等連接詞與相關連接詞]]。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項同時有 despite 與 although、because of 與 because，解題三步：① 看空格右邊緊接的內容 ② 找有沒有獨立的「主詞 + 限定動詞」——只有名詞、代名詞、V-ing 就選介系詞，有 S + V 就選連接詞，緊接逗號選連接副詞（however、therefore）③ 剩兩個以上再比語意。這是介系詞章出現率最高的題型，每回 1～2 題。詳見 [[文法17 副詞子句]] 考點 1。

### 考點 3：高頻片語介系詞
兩個字以上構成的介系詞，後面接名詞。

| 類型 | 片語 |
|---|---|
| 關於 | regarding, concerning, about, as to, as for, with regard to, in terms of, in relation to |
| 依據 | according to, in accordance with, in compliance with, based on, in line with |
| 添加 | in addition to, besides, along with, together with, as well as, apart from |
| 排除 | except for, other than, aside from, excluding |
| 替代 | instead of, in place of, rather than, on behalf of |
| 目的 | for the purpose of, in an effort to, with a view to |
| 因果 | as a result of, thanks to, on account of |
| 其他 | in charge of, in favor of, in response to, in the process of, in case of |

- **According to** the report, sales rose 5%.
- **In addition to** the salary, employees receive stock options.
- Ms. Chen will attend the meeting **on behalf of** the CEO.
- **In response to** customer feedback, we redesigned the app.

> [!warning] 注意
> - as well as 連接兩個名詞時，動詞單複數跟第一個名詞。
> - in charge of、in favor of 等固定片語，介系詞不可換。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格考片語中的一個字（in ______ of、according ______），或考整個片語（選項四個片語介系詞）。後者要看語意：關於、依據、添加、替代。

### 考點 4：動詞、形容詞、名詞的固定搭配介系詞
| 動詞 + 介系詞 | 形容詞 + 介系詞 | 名詞 + 介系詞 |
|---|---|---|
| depend on, rely on | be responsible for | access to |
| comply with | be eligible for | demand for |
| participate in | be familiar with | increase in、decrease in |
| apply for（申請）、apply to（適用） | be aware of | interest in |
| respond to, reply to | be capable of | reason for |
| consist of | be subject to | solution to |
| result in（導致）、result from（起因於） | be committed to | contribution to |
| account for | be interested in | experience in |
| specialize in | be satisfied with | effect on、impact on |
| benefit from | be concerned about | advantage of |
| contribute to | be entitled to | approval of |
| deal with, cope with | be compatible with | request for |

- The delay **resulted from** a shortage of parts.
- All staff must **comply with** the safety code.
- We have seen an **increase in** demand.
- Ms. Ito is **responsible for** overseas accounts.

> [!warning] 注意
> - result in（導致）與 result from（起因於）方向相反。
> - apply for a job、apply to a company、apply to（適用於）。
> - an increase in sales（銷售的增加）、an increase of 5%（增加了 5%）。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格考搭配的介系詞，選項四個介系詞。無法靠位置判斷，只能背。這類題每回 1～2 題，是介系詞章最需要記憶的部分，詳見 [[詞彙09 固定單字搭配的慣用語]]。

### 考點 5：其他常考介系詞
| 介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| with | 帶有、用、與 | a room **with** a view；**with** care |
| without | 沒有 | **without** prior notice |
| of | 的、關於 | a copy **of** the report |
| for | 為了、給、對 | a gift **for** you；**for** sale |
| through | 透過、經由 | **through** our website |
| against | 反對、對抗 | vote **against** the proposal |
| beyond | 超過 | **beyond** our control |
| per | 每 | $50 **per** person |
| like、unlike | 像、不像 | **Unlike** last year, … |
| as | 作為（身分） | work **as** a consultant |

> [!warning] 注意
> - as（作為）與 like（像）不同：as a manager 是「身為經理」，like a manager 是「像經理一樣」。
> - beyond 常用於 beyond repair、beyond our control。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 這類題看語意，空格後接名詞、選項是四個不同意思的介系詞。先確定句子在說「目的、方式、身分、對比」哪一種。

### 考點 6：on 與 upon
upon 是 on 的正式體，兩者在「一…就」這個用法上可以完全互換，商用文書（多益的主場）偏好 upon。差別在覆蓋範圍：on 什麼都能接，upon 幾乎只用在「一…就」這個時間用法，不拿來講日期或地點——沒有 upon Monday、upon the third floor 這種說法。

| 用法 | on | upon |
|---|---|---|
| 日期、星期 | on Monday、on May 5 | ✗ |
| 表面、樓層、街道 | on the third floor | ✗ |
| 關於、依據 | a report **on** safety、based **on** the data | ✗ |
| 一…就、當…時 | **on** arrival | **upon** arrival（更正式，多益常見） |

**核心用法：on／upon + 動作名詞（或 V-ing）＝「一…就」「當…的時候」**

這是把一個完整子句壓縮成介系詞片語的寫法，等於 as soon as／when + S + V：

- **Upon your departure from the hotel**, please return the key card.
  ＝ **As soon as you depart** from the hotel, please return the key card.
- **On receiving** the invoice, we processed the payment immediately.
  ＝ **When we received** the invoice, we processed the payment immediately.
- All items are inspected **upon delivery**.

後面接的名詞通常是動詞變來的「動作名詞」，多益高頻的有：

| 動詞 | 動作名詞 | 常見片語 |
|---|---|---|
| arrive | arrival | upon arrival、on arrival at the airport |
| depart | departure | upon your departure from the hotel |
| receive | receipt | upon receipt of payment |
| complete | completion | upon completion of the project |
| approve | approval | upon approval by the manager |
| request | request | available **upon request** |
| register | registration | upon registration |
| cancel | cancellation | upon cancellation |
| submit | submission | upon submission of the form |
| expire | expiration | upon expiration of the contract |
| retire | retirement | upon his retirement |

動作的執行者用所有格放在名詞前面：upon **your** departure、on **his** arrival、upon **the client's** approval。

**on 的其他常考搭配**

| 片語 | 意思 |
|---|---|
| on time／in time | 準時／及時（趕得上） |
| on schedule、behind schedule | 按進度／落後進度 |
| on behalf of | 代表 |
| on request、on demand | 應要求 |
| on a first-come, first-served basis | 先到先服務 |
| on duty、on leave、on site | 值班／休假中／在現場 |
| on display、on sale、on hold | 展示中／特價中／暫停 |
| depend on、rely on、focus on、work on、based on | 動詞固定搭配（見考點 4） |

> [!warning] 注意
> 1. on／upon 是介系詞，後面只能接名詞或 V-ing。要接子句（S + V）必須換成 when、as soon as、once：錯誤 upon you depart from the hotel。
> 2. on time（準時，不早不晚）與 in time（及時，趕在期限前）意思不同，多益會拿來混。
> 3. upon 沒有「在…上面」的地點意思（once upon a time 是固定敘事用語，不算）。
> 4. 「一…就」的用法兩者可互換（inspected **upon** delivery ＝ inspected **on** delivery），差別只在 upon 較正式，所以多益不會把 on 與 upon 放在同一題對立，對立的永遠是 when／as soon as／once。但已固定的片語不能換：cash **on** delivery、payable **on** delivery、**on** time、**on** schedule、**on** duty 只有 on 版本；on request 與 upon request 則都通。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格後面是 arrival、receipt、completion、approval、request 這類動作名詞，句意是「一…就」：選 upon 或 on，選項裡的 when、as soon as、once 都要接子句，直接刪掉。反過來，空格後是完整子句就選 when／as soon as／once。這條和考點 2 的「看空格右邊」是同一招。

## 實戰練習

1. All applications must be submitted ______ March 31 to be considered.
   (A) until (B) by (C) since (D) for
2. ______ the heavy rain, the outdoor event was held as scheduled.
   (A) Although (B) Despite (C) Because (D) Even
3. The new headquarters is located ______ the third floor of the Meridian Building.
   (A) in (B) at (C) on (D) by
4. The decline in sales resulted ______ increased competition from online retailers.
   (A) in (B) from (C) to (D) of
5. ______ the terms of the contract, payment is due within 30 days of delivery.
   (A) According to (B) In addition to (C) Instead of (D) On behalf of

**解答與解析**
1. (B) submit 是一次性動作，期限用 by。考點 1。
2. (B) 空格後是名詞 the heavy rain，選介系詞 Despite。考點 2。
3. (C) 樓層用 on。基礎文法地點表。
4. (B) result from 表「起因於」。考點 4。
5. (A) 「根據合約條款」用 According to。考點 3。
