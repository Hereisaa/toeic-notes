# 文法07 to 不定詞

> [!info] 藍本 Section 3 〈動狀詞〉Chapter 07。to 不定詞是「to + 原形動詞」，能當名詞、形容詞、副詞用。Part 5 每回約 1～2 題，多半考「哪些動詞、名詞、形容詞後面要接 to V」。

## 基礎文法

to 不定詞由 **to + 動詞原形** 組成，它保留動詞的性質（可以接受詞、可以被副詞修飾），但在句子裡扮演名詞、形容詞或副詞的角色。理解它的三種角色，就能判斷空格該不該填 to V。

| 角色 | 位置 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 名詞（主詞、受詞、補語） | 動詞前、及物動詞後、be 動詞後 | Our goal is **to expand** into Asia. |
| 形容詞（修飾名詞） | 名詞後面 | We need more time **to review** the contract. |
| 副詞（表目的、結果、原因） | 句首或句尾 | She called **to confirm** the appointment. |

- **To reduce** costs, the company merged two departments.（副詞，表目的）
- The manager decided **to postpone** the launch.（名詞，當 decided 的受詞）
- There are several issues **to discuss** at the meeting.（形容詞，修飾 issues）

to 不定詞的主詞若與句子主詞不同，用 **for + 受詞 + to V** 表示：It is important **for employees to attend** the training.

否定放在 to 前面：We agreed **not to disclose** the figures.

## 必考文法

### 考點 1：特定動詞後面只能接 to V
有些動詞的受詞一定是 to 不定詞，不能是動名詞。這類動詞多半帶有「意願、計畫、決定、期望」的意思。

| 類型 | 動詞 |
|---|---|
| 意願 | want, wish, hope, would like, desire |
| 計畫 | plan, intend, aim, decide, choose |
| 期望承諾 | expect, promise, agree, offer, refuse |
| 嘗試努力 | attempt, strive, manage, fail, afford |
| 其他 | tend, hesitate, seek, need |

- The board **agreed to approve** the budget.
- We **intend to hire** three new engineers.
- The supplier **failed to deliver** on time.

> [!warning] 注意
> - manage to V 是「設法做到」，不是「管理」。
> - afford 通常與 can／cannot 連用：We cannot afford to lose this client.
> - need to V（需要做）與 need V-ing（需要被做，被動意）不同：The printer needs repairing。
> - **不能用「事情還沒發生」當判斷依據**：consider、postpone、avoid 後面的事同樣沒發生，卻只能接 V-ing。這兩章的分工是「朝目標前進（to V）」vs「把那件事當成審視對象（V-ing）」，完整說明見 [[文法08 動名詞]] 考點 1。
> - 意思相近但句型相反的一組：expect **to V** vs anticipate **V-ing**。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項是同一動詞的四種形式（to submit／submitting／submitted／submit），空格前是上表動詞，直接選 to V。判斷時間約 5 秒，不必讀完整句。

### 考點 2：動詞 + 受詞 + to V
有些動詞後面先接受詞（人或物），再接 to 不定詞，表示「要求、允許、使某人做某事」。

| 動詞 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| ask, require, request | The client **asked us to revise** the quote. |
| allow, permit, enable | The new system **enables staff to work** remotely. |
| encourage, urge, advise | We **encourage employees to participate**. |
| expect, want, would like | We **expect the shipment to arrive** by Friday. |
| remind, instruct, invite | Please **remind him to sign** the form. |
| cause, force, persuade | The delay **forced us to reschedule**. |

> [!warning] 注意
> - 這類動詞改成被動時，to V 保留：Employees **are required to wear** ID badges.
> - make、let、have + 受詞 + **原形**（不加 to）：The manager **let us leave** early.
> - help + 受詞 + (to) V，to 可省略。三類使役／致使動詞的完整對照見 [[文法03 動詞的形態與種類]] 考點 5。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 常以被動形式出現：be required、expected、allowed、asked、encouraged、advised + ______，空格選 to V。看到「be + p.p. + 空格」且 p.p. 是上表動詞，就是 to V。

### 考點 3：特定名詞後面接 to V
抽象名詞後面接 to 不定詞當形容詞修飾，說明「做某事的能力、機會、權利、計畫」。

- ability, capacity, effort, attempt, plan, decision, opportunity, chance, right, way, time, need, willingness, intention, permission, authority

- The firm has the **ability to adapt** quickly.
- This is a good **opportunity to expand** our network.
- Employees have the **right to request** a review.

> [!warning] 注意
> - 這些名詞多數也可接 of + V-ing 或 for + N，但多益幾乎只考 to V。
> - 名詞前的形容詞不影響判斷：a great **opportunity to** learn。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格在名詞後面，選項有 to V、V-ing、p.p.、原形。若名詞在上表，選 to V；若名詞不在上表且空格後面沒有受詞，考慮分詞（見 [[文法09 分詞]]）。

### 考點 4：特定形容詞後面接 to V
be + 形容詞 + to V，表示「能夠、願意、可能、準備好做某事」。

- able, unable, likely, unlikely, eligible, ready, willing, reluctant, eager, pleased, glad, sure, free, due, scheduled, supposed, bound, entitled

- The results **are likely to improve** next quarter.
- All full-time employees **are eligible to apply**.
- The report **is due to be released** on Monday.

> [!warning] 注意
> - be scheduled、due、supposed + to V 表示預定或即將發生，常與未來時間連用。
> - be about to V（即將）也是 be + … + to V 的形式，但 about 是介系詞不是形容詞，單獨記。
> - be used to V-ing（習慣）是例外，to 是介系詞，見 [[文法08 動名詞]]。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格在 be + 形容詞後面，選 to V。另一種考法是空格考形容詞本身：Employees are ______ to receive a bonus. (A) eligible (B) eligibility，看到後面的 to V 選形容詞。

### 考點 5：表目的的 to V 與 in order to、so as to
to V 放在句首或句尾，表示「為了…」。加上 in order 或 so as 意思相同但更正式。

- **To meet** the deadline, the team worked overtime.
- We upgraded the software **in order to improve** security.
- Please arrive early **so as to avoid** delays.

> [!warning] 注意
> - 否定：in order **not** to、so as **not** to。
> - so that + 子句（有主詞動詞）與 so as to + V 不同，見 [[文法17 副詞子句]]。
> - 句首 To V 後面要有逗號再接主句。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 句首空格、後面接原形動詞、逗號後是完整句：選 To 或 In order to。選項若同時有 In order to 與 So that，看空格後是動詞（選 In order to）還是子句（選 So that）。

### 考點 6：too … to、enough to、be to 用法
- too + 形／副 + to V：太…而不能。The box is **too heavy to lift**.
- 形／副 + enough + to V：夠…可以。The room is **large enough to hold** 50 people.
- be + to V：預定、命令、可能。The CEO **is to visit** the plant next week.

> [!warning] 注意
> - enough 放在形容詞或副詞**後面**，放在名詞**前面**：enough time、good enough。
> - be to V 在多益多當「預定」解，等於 be scheduled to。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 看到 too 或 enough，後面空格選 to V；看到 enough 與形容詞並列考位置，形容詞在前。

## 實戰練習

1. The marketing team has decided ______ the product launch until March.
   (A) postpone (B) postponing (C) to postpone (D) postponed
2. All visitors are required ______ a badge while in the building.
   (A) wear (B) wearing (C) to wear (D) worn
3. We appreciate the opportunity ______ our services to your company.
   (A) present (B) to present (C) presenting (D) presented
4. ______ customer satisfaction, the company extended its return policy.
   (A) Improve (B) Improving (C) To improve (D) Improved
5. The new warehouse is large ______ to store twice the current inventory.
   (A) enough (B) too (C) so (D) very

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (C) decide 只接 to V。考點 1。
> 2. (C) be required + to V。考點 2。
> 3. (B) opportunity 後接 to V。考點 3。
> 4. (C) 句首表目的、逗號後是完整句，選 To improve。考點 5。
> 5. (A) 形容詞 large + enough + to V。考點 6。
