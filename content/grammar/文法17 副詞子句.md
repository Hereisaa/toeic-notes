# 文法17 副詞子句

> [!info] 藍本 Section 5〈連接詞與子句〉Chapter 17。副詞子句由從屬連接詞引導，說明主句的時間、原因、條件、讓步。Part 5 每回約 2～3 題，Part 6 每篇幾乎都有一題轉折語，是連接詞類題目的大宗。

## 基礎文法
副詞子句是「主詞 + 動詞」的完整子句，前面掛一個從屬連接詞（because、although、when、if 等），整個子句當副詞用，修飾主句。副詞子句可以放在主句前面（後面加逗號）或主句後面（不加逗號）。

- **Because** the shipment was delayed, the launch was postponed.
- The launch was postponed **because** the shipment was delayed.

多益最愛考的是：三種長得像、功能不同的字。

| 類別 | 後面接什麼 | 例子 | 句子結構 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 從屬連接詞 | 子句（S + V） | because、although、if、when | 連接詞 + S + V, S + V |
| 介系詞 | 名詞、動名詞 | because of、despite、during | 介系詞 + N, S + V |
| 連接副詞 | 獨立句子 | however、therefore、moreover | S + V. 連接副詞, S + V |

三類依邏輯分組的對照表：

| 邏輯 | 從屬連接詞（+ 子句） | 介系詞（+ 名詞） | 連接副詞（獨立句） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 因果 | because、since、as、now that、so that | because of、due to、owing to、thanks to | therefore、thus、consequently、as a result、accordingly |
| 讓步、對比 | although、though、even though、even if、while、whereas | despite、in spite of、notwithstanding | however、nevertheless、nonetheless、on the other hand、in contrast |
| 條件 | if、unless、provided (that)、as long as、in case、only if、once | in case of、in the event of、without | otherwise |
| 時間 | when、while、as、before、after、until、as soon as、by the time、whenever | during、before、after、prior to、following、upon | meanwhile、then、afterward、subsequently |
| 補充 | — | in addition to、besides、along with | moreover、furthermore、in addition、also |

- **Although** the price rose, demand remained strong.（連接詞 + 子句）
- **Despite** the price increase, demand remained strong.（介系詞 + 名詞）
- The price rose. **However**, demand remained strong.（連接副詞 + 獨立句）

## 必考文法
### 考點 1：只看空格右邊，一秒決定詞性
這是連接詞題的第一步，也是整章 CP 值最高的一條。不必讀懂句意，只看空格緊接著的是什麼：

| 空格右邊 | 選什麼 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 句子（主詞 + 動詞） | 從屬連接詞 | **Because** the elevator is under repair, please use the stairs. |
| 名詞、動名詞 | 介系詞 | **Because of** the elevator repair, please use the stairs. |
| 逗號 | 連接副詞 | The elevator is under repair; **therefore**, please use the stairs. |

第三條的「逗號」指的是空格**緊接著**逗號（空格, S + V），這是連接副詞的招牌長相：前面一定有句號或分號隔開，自己後面帶一個逗號。

> [!warning] 注意
> 1. 逗號線索要看它「貼不貼著空格」。Despite **the delay**, the launch went ahead. 這句也有逗號，但逗號在名詞片語後面、不是貼著空格，所以選介系詞不是連接副詞。
> 2. however、therefore 用逗號直接接兩個子句是錯的（comma splice）：錯誤 The price rose, however demand remained strong.
> 3. because 與 so 不能同時出現；although 與 but 不能同時出現。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 四個選項混合 although、despite、however、in spite of 時：先看空格右邊是子句、名詞還是逗號，一次刪掉三分之二的選項，再看邏輯。這是 Part 5 連接詞題最常見的出法。

### 考點 2：時間與條件副詞子句用現在式代替未來式
when、as soon as、once、until、before、after、if、unless 引導的子句，即使指未來的事，動詞也用現在式，主句才用未來式。

| 子句 | 主句 |
|---|---|
| When the shipment **arrives** | we **will notify** you |
| As soon as the contract **is** signed | work **will begin** |
| Unless the payment **is received** by Friday | the order **will be canceled** |

- Once the audit **is complete**, the results **will be published**.
- Please wait until the manager **returns**.

> [!warning] 注意
> 這條規則只適用於副詞子句。when、if 引導名詞子句時可以用未來式：I don't know when he **will arrive**.（詳見 [[文法18 名詞子句]]）

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格在 when／once／until／if 後面，選項有 will + V 與現在式：選現在式。主句有 will 是最強的線索。

### 考點 3：原因與結果的連接詞
because、since、as、now that 都表原因，後接子句。so that 表目的，後面常有 can／could／will／may。so … that 表結果。

- **Since** the office will be closed on Friday, the deadline has been extended.
- **Now that** the merger is complete, the two teams will be combined.
- We arrived early **so that** we could set up the equipment.
- The demand was **so** high **that** the product sold out in a day.

> [!warning] 注意
> since 也可表「自從」（配現在完成式）；as 也可表「當…時」「如同」，要看語意。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格後面有 can／could 的子句，多半是 so that；前後句是「原因、結果」關係且都是子句，選 because／since。

### 考點 4：讓步與對比
although、though、even though 表「雖然」，後接子句。while、whereas 表「然而、而」，用來對比兩件事。even if 表「即使」（假設）。

- **Although** the product is expensive, it sells well.
- The Taipei branch grew rapidly, **whereas** the Tainan branch stayed flat.
- **Even if** the price drops, we will not change suppliers.

> [!warning] 注意
> 1. while 有兩個意思：時間（當…時）與對比（然而）。whereas 只表對比。
> 2. though 可以放在句尾當副詞：It is expensive, **though**.

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 前後子句語意相反，空格在句首且後接子句：選 although／though／even though／while。若後面是名詞，選 despite／in spite of。

### 考點 5：條件連接詞
if 是基本條件；unless 等於 if … not；provided (that)、providing (that)、as long as 表「只要」；only if 表「只有…才」（唯一條件，比 if 更嚴格）；in case 表「以防萬一」；once 表「一旦」。

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| if | 如果 | **If** you order today, you will receive a discount. |
| unless | 除非 | The order will ship Monday **unless** we hear otherwise. |
| provided that / as long as | 只要 | You may work remotely **as long as** you attend weekly meetings. |
| only if | 只有…才 | Refunds are issued **only if** the receipt is presented. |
| in case | 以防 | Bring an umbrella **in case** it rains. |
| once | 一旦 | **Once** payment is confirmed, the ticket will be issued. |

> [!warning] 注意
> 1. unless 後面已含否定，不再加 not：錯誤 unless you don't pay。
> 2. in case + 子句；in case of + 名詞。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項有 if／unless／although／because，語意「除非」選 unless，「只要」選 as long as、provided that。看主句是否為否定結果可判斷 unless。

### 考點 6：時間連接詞的細微差別
when（當）、while（在…期間，多配進行式）、as soon as（一…就）、until（直到）、by the time（到…時候，配完成式）、since（自從，配現在完成式）。

- **While** the manager was away, the assistant handled all inquiries.
- **By the time** the guests arrived, the room had been set up.
- The company has doubled its revenue **since** it went public.

> [!warning] 注意
> by the time + 過去式，主句用過去完成式；by the time + 現在式，主句用未來完成式。詳見 [[文法06 時態與假設語氣]]。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主句是完成式，空格後接子句：選 by the time（完成）或 since（現在完成）。主句是進行式：選 while。

### 考點 7：副詞子句的省略
副詞子句的主詞與主句主詞相同時，可以省略主詞和 be 動詞，留下分詞或形容詞。

- **When** (you are) **applying** for the position, please include two references.
- **Although** (it was) **delayed**, the shipment arrived intact.
- **If** (it is) **necessary**, we will extend the deadline.

> [!warning] 注意
> 省略後的分詞要看主被動：主句主詞主動做動作用 V-ing，被動用 p.p.。詳見 [[文法09 分詞]]。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 連接詞後面直接接空格，空格後面沒有主詞，選項是 V-ing／p.p.：看主句主詞決定主被動。

## 實戰練習
1. ______ the new machinery was expensive, it has reduced production costs significantly.
   (A) Despite (B) Although (C) However (D) In spite of
2. The technician will replace the part as soon as it ______.
   (A) arrives (B) will arrive (C) arrived (D) arriving
3. Employees must submit their expense reports by Friday; ______, they will not be reimbursed this month.
   (A) unless (B) otherwise (C) in case (D) provided that
4. The conference was postponed ______ the keynote speaker was unable to travel.
   (A) because of (B) due to (C) because (D) therefore
5. ______ the payment is received, the order will be processed within two business days.
   (A) Once (B) Despite (C) Meanwhile (D) During

**解答與解析**
1. (B) 空格後是子句 the new machinery was expensive，語意讓步，用 although，考點 1、4。
2. (A) as soon as 引導的時間副詞子句用現在式代替未來式，考點 2。
3. (B) 分號後接獨立句，需要連接副詞，語意「否則」，用 otherwise，考點 1、5。
4. (C) 空格後是子句，表原因，用 because；because of／due to 只能接名詞，考點 1、3。
5. (A) 空格後是子句，語意「一旦」，用 once；despite 與 during 接名詞，meanwhile 是連接副詞，考點 5。
