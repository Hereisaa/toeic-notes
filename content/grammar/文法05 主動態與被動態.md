# 文法05 主動態與被動態

> [!info] 藍本 Section 2〈動詞片語〉Chapter 05。主動態是主詞做動作，被動態是主詞承受動作。多益 Part 5 每回約 2～3 題，選項通常是同一動詞的主動與被動各種時態，判斷關鍵只有一個：空格後面有沒有受詞。

## 基礎文法

被動態的形式是「be + 過去分詞」，原本的受詞變成主詞，原本的主詞若要保留就放在 by 後面。只有及物動詞才有被動態，因為被動需要一個受詞來當新主詞。

| 主動 | 被動 |
|---|---|
| The board **approved** the plan. | The plan **was approved** (by the board). |
| We **will ship** the order tomorrow. | The order **will be shipped** tomorrow. |
| Someone **has fixed** the printer. | The printer **has been fixed**. |

多益商務文章大量使用被動，因為誰做的不重要，重點是「事情被完成了」。常見情境：文件被提交、產品被運送、會議被延期、員工被要求。

- All applications **must be received** by March 31.
- The conference **has been rescheduled** to next month.

## 必考文法

### 考點 1：空格後沒有受詞選被動，有受詞選主動
及物動詞後面若直接接名詞受詞，用主動；若後面是介系詞、副詞、句點或 by，用被動。

- The company **launched** a new product.（後接受詞 → 主動）
- A new product **was launched** last week.（後接時間副詞 → 被動）
- The invoice **was sent** to the client.（後接介系詞 → 被動）

> [!warning] 注意
> 授與動詞（give、offer、send、award）被動後仍可能接一個名詞：The employees **were given** a bonus. 這是被動保留第二個受詞，不是主動。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項為 launched / was launched / launching / has launched。先看空格後有無受詞，再看時間線索定時態。

### 考點 2：被動態的各種時態
被動的時態變化全部發生在 be 動詞上，過去分詞不變。

| 時態 | 被動形式 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 現在簡單 | am／is／are + p.p. | Orders **are processed** within 24 hours. |
| 過去簡單 | was／were + p.p. | The contract **was signed** yesterday. |
| 未來 | will be + p.p. | Results **will be announced** Friday. |
| 現在完成 | has／have been + p.p. | The issue **has been resolved**. |
| 過去完成 | had been + p.p. | The room **had been reserved** before we called. |
| 現在進行 | is／are being + p.p. | The lobby **is being renovated**. |
| 情態助動詞 | must／should／can be + p.p. | Reports **must be submitted** monthly. |
| to 不定詞 | to be + p.p. | The file needs **to be updated**. |

> [!warning] 注意
> 進行被動是 be being p.p.，不是 be p.p.-ing。完成被動是 have been p.p.，中間的 been 不能省略。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項有 is renovated、is renovating、is being renovated、has renovated，看空格後無受詞排除主動，再用 currently 這類線索選進行被動。

### 考點 3：不及物動詞沒有被動態
沒有受詞的動詞無法變被動。看到 was occurred、was arrived 直接排除。

| 不及物動詞 | 正確 | 錯誤 |
|---|---|---|
| occur、happen、take place | The meeting **took place** Monday. | was taken place ✗ |
| arrive、depart | The shipment **arrived** early. | was arrived ✗ |
| rise、fall、increase（不及物用法） | Prices **rose** 5%. | were risen ✗ |
| remain、exist、expire | The offer **expires** Friday. | is expired ✗ |
| appear、disappear、seem | The error **appeared** again. | was appeared ✗ |

> [!warning] 注意
> increase、decrease、open、close、begin 可及物也可不及物：Sales increased（不及物）／We increased sales（及物）。判斷要看後面有無受詞。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 選項有 occurred 與 was occurred，或 arrived 與 was arrived。不及物動詞一律選主動。

### 考點 4：被動 + 固定介系詞
許多被動片語後面接特定介系詞而非 by，多益常挖介系詞或動詞。

| 片語 | 中譯 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be located in／at | 位於 | The office **is located in** the city center. |
| be equipped with | 配備 | Each room **is equipped with** a projector. |
| be committed／dedicated to | 致力於 | We **are committed to** quality. |
| be known for | 以…聞名 | The firm **is known for** its service. |
| be involved in | 參與 | She **was involved in** the merger. |
| be interested in | 對…有興趣 | Those **interested in** the role should apply. |
| be responsible for | 負責 | He **is responsible for** logistics. |
| be based on | 以…為基礎 | Pay **is based on** performance. |
| be satisfied／pleased with | 滿意 | Clients **are satisfied with** the results. |
| be concerned about | 擔心 | Staff **are concerned about** the changes. |
| be exposed to | 暴露於 | Workers **were exposed to** noise. |
| be accustomed to | 習慣於 | We **are accustomed to** tight deadlines. |

> [!warning] 注意
> be committed to、be accustomed to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接名詞或 V-ing，不接原形動詞。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格是介系詞，前面是 be + p.p.。背熟搭配表，看到 equipped 選 with、located 選 in／at。

### 考點 5：be required／expected／scheduled／allowed + to V
表「被要求、被預期、被安排、被允許」的被動片語後面接 to 不定詞。

- All employees **are required to** wear ID badges.
- The delivery **is expected to** arrive Monday.
- The event **is scheduled to** begin at nine.
- Visitors **are not allowed to** enter the lab.

> [!warning] 注意
> 這類動詞主動時是「動詞 + 受詞 + to V」（require employees to wear），被動後受詞變主詞，to V 保留。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 空格在 be required／expected 後，選項有 to attend、attending、attend、attended，選 to V。

### 考點 6：情緒動詞的主動與被動
interest、satisfy、please、excite、disappoint、surprise 等情緒動詞，主詞是「感受者（人）」用被動 -ed，主詞是「引起感受的事物」用主動 -ing。

- Customers **were satisfied** with the service.（人，被動）
- The service **was satisfying**.（事物，主動）
- We **are pleased** to announce the new partnership.

> [!warning] 注意
> 這條規則也適用於分詞當形容詞（interested customers／an interesting offer），詳見 [[文法09 分詞]]。

> [!tip] 多益出題方向
> 主詞是人、空格後接 with／in／to，選 -ed 形式；主詞是事物，選 -ing 形式。

## 實戰練習

1. The quarterly newsletter ______ to all subscribers on the first business day of each month.
   (A) sends (B) is sent (C) sending (D) has sent
2. The new safety procedures ______ by the committee last Tuesday.
   (A) approved (B) were approving (C) were approved (D) have approved
3. The annual shareholders meeting ______ on June 12 at the downtown convention center.
   (A) was taken place (B) took place (C) has been taken place (D) is taken place
4. All conference rooms on the fourth floor ______ with video-conferencing equipment.
   (A) equip (B) are equipping (C) are equipped (D) equipped
5. Employees ______ to complete the online training module before the end of the month.
   (A) require (B) are required (C) requiring (D) have required

**解答與解析**
1. (B)：空格後是 to all subscribers，沒有受詞，選被動 is sent（考點 1）。
2. (C)：空格後是 by the committee，被動且過去時間，選 were approved（考點 1、2）。
3. (B)：take place 是不及物動詞片語，沒有被動，選 took place（考點 3）。
4. (C)：be equipped with 固定搭配（考點 4）。
5. (B)：be required to V，主詞 Employees 是被要求者（考點 5）。
