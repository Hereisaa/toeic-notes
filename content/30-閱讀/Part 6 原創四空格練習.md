# Part 6 原創四空格練習

> [!info] 原創教學練習，共 2 組 8 題
> 每組先限時 2 分 30 秒，不查字；解答預設收合。這些短題組用來練動作，不具官方試題的難度校準，不能換算分數。

## 第一組：延後出貨通知

**To:** Office Supply Customers
**Subject:** Your desk order

Thank you for ordering a desk from Harbor Furniture. Our supplier has informed us that several metal components (1) ______ delayed by a factory shutdown last week. Your desk will therefore leave our warehouse on November 12 instead of November 8.

We understand that some customers need a desk sooner. (2) ______, we can offer a loan desk at no extra charge. (3) ______. Please indicate your preferred delivery time when you call.

Once the original desk is ready, we will contact you to arrange an exchange. We appreciate your (4) ______ while we complete your order.

### 題目

1. (A) were　(B) will be　(C) are being　(D) have
2. (A) Nevertheless　(B) Therefore　(C) Similarly　(D) Otherwise
3. (A) Our factory opened ten years ago.
   (B) The metal components are used in several products.
   (C) To request one, call our customer service team by Friday.
   (D) The new catalog includes chairs in four colors.
4. (A) patient　(B) patiently　(C) patience　(D) patients

作答紀錄：用時＿＿；答案＿＿／＿＿／＿＿／＿＿；最猶豫的題號＿＿。

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> **1 A｜時態與被動。** last week 與工廠停工構成過去事件；components 是被延誤的對象，所以 were delayed。B 未來不符、C 正在進行不符這裡的過去事件、D have delayed 會變主動且缺適當受詞。
>
> **2 B｜結果。** 顧客需要更早拿到桌子，因此提供借用桌。A 表示反差，此處是需求與解法；C 沒有相似的前一個例子；D 是「否則」，沒有對應的前提要求。
>
> **3 C｜前後雙向銜接。** one 接回 loan desk，call 讓下一句 when you call 有依據。A、B 雖與公司／產品相關，卻接不上後句打電話；D 引入椅子，偏離需求。
>
> **4 C｜受詞。** appreciate your 後面需要名詞，patience 是耐心。A 是形容詞或病人單數，B 是副詞，D 是病人複數，不符語意。
>
> **複盤重點**：第 3 題若只看前句，會覺得 B 也在談零件；讀後句才能找到更強的銜接證據。

## 第二組：培訓地點異動

**To:** New Employees
**Subject:** Friday's orientation

The orientation session was originally scheduled for Room 204. However, maintenance work in that room (5) ______ until Friday afternoon, so the session will take place in Room 310.

Room 310 is on the third floor of the same building. (6) ______. These signs will be placed near the elevators before the session begins.

Please bring your employee identification card. Visitors without a card must register at reception (7) ______ entering the training area. The session will begin at 9 A.M. as planned, (8) ______ the change of location.

### 題目

5. (A) continued　(B) will continue　(C) had continued　(D) continuing
6. (A) Directional signs will help you find the new room.
   (B) The new employees have already completed their training.
   (C) Lunch is available at the café across the street.
   (D) Several employees prefer online meetings.
7. (A) during　(B) because　(C) before　(D) although
8. (A) although　(B) despite　(C) because　(D) unless

作答紀錄：用時＿＿；答案＿＿／＿＿／＿＿／＿＿；最猶豫的題號＿＿。

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> **5 B｜事件時間線。** 通知談即將到來的星期五培訓，維修將持續到下午，所以 will continue。A、C 是過去時態，與本段未來安排不合；D 單獨不能當此子句的完整動詞。
>
> **6 A｜後句指涉。** These signs 需要可辨識的標誌；A 引入 directional signs 並承接新房間。B 說已完成培訓，與即將舉行不合；C、D 都無法讓 signs 自然接續。
>
> **7 C｜動作順序。** 先登記再進入，before entering。A during 不直接接此動名詞結構；B、D 在這裡缺合適子句，邏輯也不合。
>
> **8 B｜讓步＋名詞片語。** despite the change 表達地點雖改，開始時間不變。A although、C because、D unless 不能在此直接接 the change of location 充當完整子句。
>
> **複盤重點**：地點改變不代表時間改變；Part 7 也常用這類「只改一個條件」的干擾選項。
