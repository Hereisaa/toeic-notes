# Part 7 原創單篇與多篇練習

> [!info] 原創教學練習：單篇 3 題、雙篇 5 題、三篇 5 題
> 每題找出證據再選；解答預設收合。篇幅刻意縮短以練推理，不代表正式試題難度，不用來估分。

## 第一組：單篇電郵｜建議 3 分鐘

**To:** All Staff
**From:** Facilities Team
**Subject:** North entrance closure

The north entrance will be closed from November 4 through November 6 while its automatic doors are replaced. During this period, employees should use the east entrance, which can be opened with the same staff access cards.

Deliveries will continue to arrive at the loading area behind the building. Please tell any visitors you are expecting to use the east entrance as well. The reception desk there will be staffed from 8 A.M. to 6 P.M.

### 題目

1. What is the main purpose of the e-mail?
   (A) To announce a change in office hours
   (B) To explain temporary access arrangements
   (C) To introduce a new delivery service
   (D) To request applications for a reception job

2. What is indicated about staff access cards?
   (A) They will be replaced on November 4.
   (B) They are required for delivery drivers.
   (C) They can be used at the east entrance.
   (D) They will stop working after 6 P.M.

3. What are employees asked to do?
   (A) Notify their visitors of the alternate entrance
   (B) Collect parcels from the reception desk
   (C) Avoid all deliveries for three days
   (D) Arrive at work before 8 A.M.

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> **1 B。** 全文交代維修期間如何進出，temporary access arrangements 是主旨改寫。A 把櫃台服務時間偷換成辦公時間；C 明說送貨照舊；D 沒有徵人。
>
> **2 C。** same staff access cards 對應既有卡片能開東門。A 是更換門而非卡；B 未要求司機用員工卡；D 把櫃台下班誤當門禁失效。
>
> **3 A。** tell any visitors … use the east entrance 對應 notify … alternate entrance。B 與 loading area 不符；C 與 continue 相反；D 將櫃台開門時間誤作員工到班要求。
>
> **定位筆記**：north entrance 的施工是背景，主要功能是通知替代進出方式；不要只挑帶有文章原字的選項。

## 第二組：雙篇｜建議 5 分鐘

### 文件 A：活動場地方案

**Riverside Meeting Center**

| Room | Maximum capacity | Daily rental |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cedar | 20 | $180 |
| Maple | 35 | $250 |
| Oak | 60 | $360 |

A projector is included in every rental. Catering costs an additional $15 per person. Reservations confirmed by November 10 receive a 10 percent discount on room rental only.

### 文件 B：確認信

**Date:** November 9
**To:** Ms. Lin
**Subject:** Your booking

We have confirmed your reservation for November 21. As requested, we have booked the least expensive room that can accommodate your group of 28. Lunch will be provided for all attendees. Your booking qualifies for our early reservation discount.

Please send your final lunch selections by November 15. You may change the selections before that date without an additional charge.

### 題目

4. Which room has Ms. Lin reserved?
   (A) Cedar　(B) Maple　(C) Oak　(D) None of the rooms

5. What is the discounted room rental fee?
   (A) $180　(B) $225　(C) $250　(D) $324

6. What is the total charge for the room and lunch for 28 people?
   (A) $603　(B) $630　(C) $645　(D) $670

7. What must Ms. Lin do by November 15?
   (A) Confirm her reservation
   (B) Submit her lunch selections
   (C) Pay for a projector
   (D) Increase the number of attendees

8. Which item is included in the room rental?
   (A) Lunch　(B) Parking　(C) A projector　(D) Printed invitations

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> **4 B｜跨文。** B 的 28 人＋最低合適價格，對應 A 的 Maple：Cedar 只能 20 人，Oak 雖放得下卻更貴，D 不符已確認預訂。
>
> **5 B｜跨文。** Maple $250 × 0.9＝$225。A 是 Cedar 原價，C 未折扣，D 是 Oak 折後價。
>
> **6 C｜跨文。** $225＋28×$15＝$645。A 的 $603 是把餐費也打九折；D $670 是完全沒折扣；B $630 不符題示費率。only 限定折扣只適用場地。
>
> **7 B｜細節。** final lunch selections by November 15。A 預訂在 11/9 已確認；C 投影機已含；D 未要求增加人數。
>
> **8 C｜細節。** projector is included。A 餐費額外計，B、D 未提及包含。
>
> **關鍵時間**：折扣看確認日期 11/9，而非活動日期 11/21；這是日期角色，不是單純比大小。

## 第三組：三篇｜建議 5 分鐘

### 文件 A：接駁時刻表

**Harbor Hotel — Airport Shuttle**

| Departure from hotel | Arrival at airport |
|---|---|
| 7:30 A.M. | 8:10 A.M. |
| 8:30 A.M. | 9:10 A.M. |
| 9:30 A.M. | 10:10 A.M. |

Seats must be reserved at the front desk by 8 P.M. on the previous evening. The shuttle is free for hotel guests.

### 文件 B：旅客訊息

**November 18, 6:10 P.M. — Elena Cruz to Front Desk**

My flight leaves at 11 A.M. tomorrow, and I need to reach the airport no later than 9 A.M. Please reserve the latest shuttle that meets that requirement. I will have one suitcase.

### 文件 C：櫃台回覆

**November 18, 6:25 P.M. — Front Desk to Elena Cruz**

Your seat is confirmed. Because of roadwork, tomorrow's shuttle trips will each take 20 minutes longer than shown in the schedule. Departure times will not change. The driver will collect you at the side entrance rather than the main lobby.

### 題目

9. Which shuttle has been reserved for Ms. Cruz?
   (A) 7:30 A.M.　(B) 8:30 A.M.　(C) 9:30 A.M.　(D) 11 A.M.

10. At what time is she now expected to reach the airport?
    (A) 8:10 A.M.　(B) 8:30 A.M.　(C) 9:10 A.M.　(D) 9:30 A.M.

11. What is unchanged despite the roadwork?
    (A) Travel time　(B) Airport arrival times　(C) Departure times　(D) The pickup location

12. Where should Ms. Cruz wait?
    (A) At the airport information desk
    (B) In the main lobby
    (C) At the front desk
    (D) At the side entrance

13. What is indicated about her reservation request?
    (A) It was made after the deadline.
    (B) It requires a payment for the shuttle.
    (C) It was made before the previous-evening deadline.
    (D) It asks to change her flight time.

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> **9 A｜跨文。** B 要 9 點前抵達；A 的 7:30 班原本 8:10 到，C 增加 20 分鐘後 8:30 到，仍符合。8:30 班改為 9:30 到，不符合；9:30 班更晚；11 點是飛機時間不是接駁班次。
>
> **10 B｜三篇整合。** 先由需求選 7:30 班，再由時刻表找 8:10，最後加更新的 20 分鐘＝8:30。A 忽略施工；C 是下一班原時間；D 是下一班更新後時間。
>
> **11 C｜變更範圍。** Departure times will not change。A、B 因路程加長而變；D 接送點由大廳改側門。
>
> **12 D｜最新指示。** C 明說 side entrance rather than main lobby。A 是目的地的其他位置；B 是被替換的地點；C front desk 是訂位處，並非上車處。
>
> **13 C｜日期＋規則。** 11/18 晚上 6:10 訂隔天的車，早於前晚 8 點。A 相反；B 接駁對住客免費；D 只是交代航班時間，未要求更改。
>
> **跨文地圖**：A＝原時間與規則；B＝個人限制；C＝更新。不是把三篇全文背起來，而是找每題需要的欄位。
