# 詞彙08 名詞慣用語

> [!info] 藍本 VOCABULARY Section 2〈慣用語〉Chapter 08。考「名詞 + 介系詞」與「介系詞 + 名詞」的固定片語，Part 5 每回約 1～2 題，Part 6 常以 in advance、on schedule 這類片語考語意。

> [!abstract]- 本章重點
> 名詞慣用語分兩個方向。第一是「名詞 + 介系詞」：名詞決定後面的介系詞，例如 access to、demand for、increase in，考法是給名詞選介系詞，或給介系詞選名詞。第二是「介系詞 + 名詞」組成的副詞或形容詞片語：in advance、on schedule、under warranty，考法多是四個介系詞片語選一個符合句意的。前者靠背搭配，後者靠背意思。這一章的名詞很多是動詞或形容詞變來的，介系詞通常跟原本的動詞一致：respond to 變 response to，demand for 沿用，interested in 變 interest in。

## 必考清單
### 名詞 + to
| 慣用語 | 中譯 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| access to | 進入、使用…的權限 | Only managers have access to the payroll system. |

| solution to | …的解決方法 | The consultant proposed a solution to the delays. |
| response to | 對…的回應 | In response to feedback, we extended our hours. |
| alternative to | …的替代方案 | Video calls are a cheap alternative to travel. |
| contribution to | 對…的貢獻 | Her contribution to the project was significant. |
| attention to | 對…的注意 | Attention to detail is essential in accounting. |
| exposure to | 接觸、曝露於 | The role offers exposure to international clients. |

## 易混淆與注意
> [!warning] 注意
> - access to 中的 access 是名詞，access 當動詞時是及物動詞，直接接受詞，不加 to。
> - increase / decrease / rise / decline **in** 接變動的項目，**of** 接數字幅度：an increase **in** sales **of** 10%。
> - in charge **of**（負責某事）與 in the charge **of**（在某人管理下）不同。
> - on time（準時）與 in time（及時、來得及）不同。
> - in advance 後面不接受詞；若要接，用 in advance **of** the meeting。
> - on behalf of 後接人或組織；in accordance with 後接規定、政策。
> - reputation **for** + 特質、reputation **as** + 身分：a reputation for quality、a reputation as a leader。
> - 這一類片語多為副詞用法，放在句首時後面加逗號：In addition to…, we…。

## 實戰練習
1. Only authorized personnel have ______ to the server room.
   (A) approach (B) access (C) entrance (D) admission
2. There has been a sharp increase ______ the number of online applications.
   (A) of (B) on (C) in (D) at
3. Ms. Ortega will attend the conference ______ behalf of the marketing director.
   (A) in (B) on (C) at (D) for
4. The warehouse expansion was completed two weeks ______ schedule.
   (A) ahead of (B) in charge of (C) as a result of (D) regardless of
5. Reservations must be canceled in ______ to avoid a penalty.
   (A) advance (B) effect (C) stock (D) person

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (B) access to 使用權限；approach 接 to 但語意不合，entrance 與 admission 接 to 時多指入場。
> 2. (C) increase in + 變動項目。
> 3. (B) on behalf of 代表。
> 4. (A) ahead of schedule 進度超前；其他選項語意不合。
> 5. (A) in advance 事先；in effect 生效，in stock 有庫存，in person 親自。
