# 詞彙11 意義相近的名詞

> [!info] 藍本 VOCABULARY Section 3〈近義詞〉Chapter 11。考中文意思相近但使用情境不同的名詞，Part 5 每回約 1～2 題，Part 7 同義詞題也常考。

> [!abstract]- 本章重點
> 近義名詞題的線索在名詞前後的搭配字。例如「費用」有 fee、charge、fare、cost，但交通用 fare、服務用 fee、額外收費用 charge；「行程」有 schedule、itinerary、agenda，但旅行用 itinerary、會議議程用 agenda。另一個線索是可數性：advice、information、equipment 不可數，不能加 a 或 s，若空格前有 an，這些字就直接排除。背的時候把「這個字用在什麼場合、可不可數」一起記。

## 必考清單
### 1. 費用
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| fee | 服務、專業或會員費 | membership / registration / late | The registration fee is $50. |
| charge | 收費、額外費用 | extra / service / free of | There is no charge for delivery. |
| fare | 交通票價 | bus / train / air | Train fares will rise in April. |
| cost | 成本、花費 | production / shipping / of living | Shipping costs are included. |
| expense | 支出（常複數） | travel / business / incur | Travel expenses are reimbursed. |

怎麼分：fare 只用於交通，fee 是收取的固定費用，expense 是公司或個人付出去的支出。

### 2. 收入與利潤
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| revenue | 公司總營收 | annual / total / generate | Annual revenue reached $2 million. |
| income | 個人或公司收入 | monthly / net / source of | Her monthly income increased. |
| profit | 利潤（收入減成本） | net / make a / margin | The firm made a small profit. |
| earnings | 盈餘、所得 | quarterly / report / per share | Quarterly earnings beat forecasts. |

怎麼分：revenue 是還沒扣成本的總收入，profit 是扣完成本的獲利，income 偏個人所得。

### 3. 折扣與退款
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| discount | 折扣 | 10% / on / offer a | Members get a discount on all items. |
| refund | 退款 | full / issue a / request a | We issued a full refund. |
| rebate | 購後返還的部分款項 | mail-in / tax / receive a | Customers receive a $20 rebate by mail. |
| reimbursement | 費用報銷 | travel / expense / claim | Submit receipts for reimbursement. |

怎麼分：discount 是付款時直接減價，rebate 是付款後退回一部分，reimbursement 是替公司墊付後拿回。

### 4. 職位與工作
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| position | 職位（正式） | apply for / fill / senior | We are seeking to fill a senior position. |
| occupation | 職業類別 | list your / by | State your occupation on the form. |
| vacancy | 職缺、空房 | fill a / job / no | There is a vacancy in the sales team. |
| career | 職涯 | pursue a / path / change | She pursued a career in finance. |

怎麼分：position 是某公司的具體職位，occupation 是行業類別，vacancy 強調空缺，career 是一生的職涯。

### 5. 資格與證照
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| qualification | 資格、學經歷 | required / academic / have the | Candidates must have the required qualifications. |
| certificate | 證書 | issue a / of completion | Participants receive a certificate of completion. |
| license | 執照 | valid / driver / renew a | A valid license is required to operate the vehicle. |
| permit | 許可證 | building / parking / obtain a | Obtain a parking permit from security. |

怎麼分：license 是從事某活動的執照，permit 是一次性或短期許可，certificate 是完成或合格的證明。

### 6. 顧客
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| customer | 買東西的顧客 | regular / satisfaction / service | Regular customers earn reward points. |
| client | 接受專業服務的客戶 | corporate / meet with / base | The lawyer met with a new client. |
| consumer | 消費者（總稱） | demand / spending / goods | Consumer spending rose in May. |
| patron | 常客、贊助者 | restaurant / library / loyal | Patrons of the library may borrow ten books. |

怎麼分：customer 是零售顧客，client 是律師、顧問等專業服務的客戶，consumer 是經濟學上的消費者。

### 7. 規定
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| regulation | 法規（政府或機構） | safety / comply with / government | Follow all safety regulations. |
| policy | 政策、方針 | company / return / adopt a | Our return policy allows 30 days. |
| guideline | 指導原則（非強制） | follow / issue / set | Follow the guidelines for submitting reports. |
| procedure | 程序、步驟 | standard / follow the / emergency | Follow the emergency procedure. |

怎麼分：regulation 有強制力，policy 是組織方針，guideline 是建議做法，procedure 是操作步驟。

### 8. 行程與議程
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| schedule | 時間表 | on / behind / tight | The project is behind schedule. |
| itinerary | 旅行行程 | travel / detailed / confirm the | Your itinerary is attached. |
| agenda | 會議議程 | meeting / on the / item | The budget is the first item on the agenda. |
| timetable | 班次時刻表 | bus / train / class | Check the train timetable online. |

怎麼分：itinerary 專指旅行，agenda 專指會議要討論的項目，timetable 是固定班次。

### 9. 會議與活動
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| conference | 大型會議 | annual / attend a / hold a | The annual conference is in Boston. |
| seminar | 專題講座 | training / attend a / on | She attended a seminar on tax law. |
| workshop | 實作工作坊 | hands-on / conduct a / on | The workshop covers spreadsheet skills. |
| session | 一節活動、時段 | training / morning / question-and-answer | The morning session starts at nine. |

怎麼分：conference 規模最大，seminar 偏聽講，workshop 偏動手，session 是活動中的一段。

### 10. 效果與影響
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| effect | 效果、結果 | have an / on / take | The ad had a positive effect on sales. |
| impact | 強烈影響 | significant / on / have an | The strike had a major impact on exports. |
| influence | 影響力（可施加） | on / under the / exert | Reviews have a strong influence on buyers. |
| outcome | 結果、成果 | successful / of / final | The outcome of the vote was announced. |

怎麼分：effect 與 impact 都接 on，impact 語氣更強；influence 強調施加影響的能力，outcome 是最終結果。

### 11. 問題與議題
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| problem | 需要解決的問題 | solve a / technical / cause a | We fixed the technical problem. |
| issue | 議題、爭議 | address the / raise an / key | The meeting addressed several key issues. |
| matter | 事情、事務 | urgent / personal / discuss the | This is an urgent matter. |
| concern | 擔憂的事 | raise / address / safety | Staff raised concerns about overtime. |

怎麼分：problem 一定是負面且要解決，issue 是待討論的議題，matter 是中性的事情，concern 帶擔憂。

### 12. 通知與公告
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| notice | 通知、告示 | prior / give / short | Please give two weeks' notice. |
| notification | 正式通知（動作或訊息） | receive a / email / of | You will receive a notification by email. |
| announcement | 宣布、公告 | make an / official / public | The CEO made an official announcement. |
| reminder | 提醒 | send a / friendly / gentle | This is a friendly reminder about the deadline. |

怎麼分：notice 常指提前告知的期間或貼出的告示，notification 是系統或機構發出的通知，announcement 是公開宣布。

### 13. 合約與協議
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| contract | 正式合約 | sign a / renew a / under | She signed a two-year contract. |
| agreement | 協議（可正式可非正式） | reach an / in / mutual | Both sides reached an agreement. |
| terms | 條款（複數） | and conditions / of payment | Read the terms and conditions carefully. |
| warranty | 保固 | under / one-year / extended | The device comes with a one-year warranty. |

怎麼分：contract 是具法律效力的文件，agreement 是雙方的共識，terms 是合約裡的條款，warranty 是產品保固。

### 14. 估價與帳單
| 單字 | 語意重點 | 常接的字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| estimate | 估價、估計 | cost / free / provide an | We provide a free estimate. |
| quote | 報價 | price / request a / get a | Request a quote before ordering. |
| invoice | 發票、請款單 | issue an / pay the / attached | The invoice is due in 30 days. |
| receipt | 收據 | keep the / upon / of | Keep your receipt for returns. |

怎麼分：estimate 與 quote 都在交易前，quote 是確定價格；invoice 是請款，receipt 是付款後的證明。

## 易混淆與注意
> [!warning] 注意
> - 不可數名詞：advice、information、equipment、furniture、luggage、merchandise、research、access。前面不能有 a 或 an，也不加 s。
> - expense 表支出時常用複數 expenses；expenditure 是正式用語，多指政府或公司的總支出。
> - fare 只用於交通票價，fee 用於服務與會員，兩者不能互換。
> - agenda 在現代英語是單數可數名詞（an agenda，複數 agendas），雖然字源是拉丁文 agendum 的複數。
> - notice 在 give notice 中不可數，在 a notice on the board 中可數。
> - effect 是名詞（影響），affect 是動詞（影響），拼字相近。
> - price 是物品的售價，cost 是取得所需的花費，value 是價值，三者不同。
> - client 與 customer 在多益中有時互換，但律師事務所、顧問公司一定用 client。

## 實戰練習
1. Passengers can now check bus ______ on the transit authority website.
   (A) fees (B) fares (C) charges (D) costs
2. A detailed ______ for the sales trip, including hotel bookings, is attached.
   (A) agenda (B) program (C) itinerary (D) timetable
3. All employees must comply with the new safety ______ issued by the ministry.
   (A) regulations (B) opinions (C) impressions (D) suggestions
4. Please keep your ______ as proof of purchase in case you need a refund.
   (A) invoice (B) estimate (C) quote (D) receipt
5. The consulting firm has expanded its ______ base to include several banks.
   (A) customer (B) client (C) consumer (D) patron

> [!success]- 解答與解析
> 1. (B) fare 專指交通票價。
> 2. (C) itinerary 專指旅行行程；agenda 是會議議程，program 是活動節目表，timetable 是班次表。
> 3. (A) regulation 由政府部門發布且有強制力，與 comply with 搭配；opinion、impression、suggestion 都沒有強制力，不能 comply with。
> 4. (D) receipt 是付款證明；invoice 是請款單，estimate 與 quote 在交易前。
> 5. (B) 顧問公司的客戶用 client；client base 是固定搭配。
